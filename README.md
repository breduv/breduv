## Selected projects

### [Hapuga](https://github.com/breduv/Hapuga)

Download music, videos, and photo galleries in Telegram. Search by song title and pick a track
to get an MP3, or send a media link. For YouTube videos, choose a resolution or save just the audio.

<sub>Python · aiogram · yt-dlp · gallery-dl · FFmpeg</sub>

### [Webwit](https://github.com/breduv/Webwit)

An AI assistant for Telegram that searches the web before answering and keeps your conversation
history. Ask a question with `/clear`. To branch from an earlier answer, reply to it with
`/clear` and your next question.

<sub>Python · aiogram · LangGraph · MCP · SQLite</sub>

### [Voxsum](https://github.com/breduv/Voxsum)

Forward a voice message to Voxsum or mention the bot in a reply to one. It transcribes the audio
locally and sends a short summary back in Telegram.

<sub>Python · aiogram · onnx-asr · FFmpeg · LangGraph</sub>

### [manga-downloader](https://github.com/breduv/manga-downloader)

A desktop app that saves public MangaLib chapters as CBZ archives for offline reading.
Paste a series link and choose which chapters to download. The app keeps a local library
and checks for new chapters. Based on [BSolonenko/manga-downloader](https://github.com/BSolonenko/manga-downloader).

[Download for Windows](https://github.com/breduv/manga-downloader/releases/latest)<br />
<sub>Python · PyQt5 · Selenium · curl_cffi</sub>
